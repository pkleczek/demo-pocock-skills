# Event Registration

This context manages public Events, the trusted Organizers who publish them, and the Registrations attendees make to attend.

## Language

### Event and registration

**Event**:
A publicly listed meeting, meetup, conference, or assembly that takes place either in person at a physical venue or online. Each Event is one registrable occurrence with a single overall start/end range (possibly multi-day); repeated meetups are separate Events, and attendees register for the Event as a whole rather than for individual sessions.

**Organizer**:
A trusted person who creates, publishes, edits, and cancels Events.

**Attendee**:
A person who registers themself to attend an Event.
_Avoid_: User

**Registration**:
A confirmed record of one Attendee signing up to attend one Event. It is free and immediate; only one active Registration per email per Event is allowed, and cancellation preserves the record while allowing later re-registration. The current scope has no capacity limit or waitlist.
_Avoid_: Ticket, booking

**Cancelled Event**:
An Event whose Organizer has cancelled it; the Event remains recorded and its active Registrations are marked as cancelled because of the Event cancellation.
